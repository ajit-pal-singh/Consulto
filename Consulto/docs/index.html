About Consulto

Consulto is an iOS application designed to help you manage your health journey. From medicine and appointment reminders to tracking your vitals (including Apple HealthKit integration for heart rate data) and keeping a secure record of your medical files and consultations, Consulto is your personalized health hub. We also utilize advanced ARKit technologies for specialized tracking features to enhance your experience.
