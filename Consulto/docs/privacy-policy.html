Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Consulto ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Consulto application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and securely handle your authentication via OTPs and passwords.
  • Health Data (Apple HealthKit): With your explicit permission, the App accesses health data from Apple HealthKit, specifically regarding your heart rate and vitals. We use this data solely to display your health trends within the App.
  • TrueDepth API and ARKit Data: The App utilizes Apple's TrueDepth camera and ARKit framework for face tracking and eye calibration features. We collect facial geometry and movement data strictly for the operation of these features.
  • User-Generated Content: We store the medical records, consultation session notes, and medication/appointment reminders that you actively input into the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality.
  • To display your health data, vitals, and reminders within the App.
  • To authenticate your identity and maintain the security of your account.

3. Apple HealthKit Data

Your health data accessed through Apple HealthKit is treated with the utmost sensitivity. We do not use or disclose your HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining purposes. Your HealthKit data is only used to provide the health tracking and visualization features within the App. You can manage the App's access to this data at any time through your device's Health app settings.

4. TrueDepth API and Face Tracking Data

Data collected via the TrueDepth API and ARKit (face tracking and eye calibration data) is processed in real-time on your device. We do not store this facial data on our servers, nor do we share it with third parties. The data is used exclusively to calibrate and operate the tracking features within the App and is discarded immediately after processing.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with our business partners. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including secure authentication protocols, to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information, username, password, transaction information, and data stored on our App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@consulto.app.
